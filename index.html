WHAT THIS DOES TO YOUR TRADES

Nothing. Not one of these gates an entry. They are on screen as weather, not as a rule.

All twenty were walk-forwarded as entry gates on the fused rule (regime_test.py). The last column is the share of 2,000 bootstrap histories in which gating on that signal beat not gating — the bar is 90%.

1 of 20 cleared. Testing twenty candidates at a 10% false-positive rate throws up 2.0 winners by chance, so one winner is fewer than chance predicts. XLU/SPY at 92.5% is what a lottery ticket looks like, and after correcting for twenty tests the bar is ~99.5%. None was adopted.

The bot’s own edge sits in 2018, 2020 and 2022 — exactly when SPY is under its long average. A regime gate deletes its best years and re-enters late. A filter that helps a dip-buyer hurts a breakout bot.
