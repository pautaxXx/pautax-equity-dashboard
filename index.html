HOW A NAME QUALIFIES FOR PKZ

1 · ZONE — on a timeframe that reads bullish (higher high and higher low), a line the price has touched 3 or more times.

2 · ALIGNED — 4 or more of the 7 frames (1 min, 5 min, 30 min, 1 hour, 4 hour, 1 day, 1 week) carry a live line touched 4+ times. The PKZ entry is the average of those lines.

3 · AT THE BUY PRICE — the live price trades 0.8% or more UNDER that entry, still above the lowest higher low and within 1.5% of the entry. The bot then places a limit buy at the entry minus 0.8%.

After the buy: it is sold as soon as 4 or more of its 7 timeframes read bearish; while the frames can be read there is no price stop under the buy (the higher-low line, never more than 1.5% under the buy, sells only if they cannot). Once it is up 1% the stop locks +0.5%; once it is up the ARM RULE amount the stop keeps that much and trails 0.3% under its high. Every position is sold by 3:50 PM. Up to 10 PKZ positions at once, each an equal share of the free cash; a resting buy order counts as one.
